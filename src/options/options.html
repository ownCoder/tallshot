What Tallshot can and cannot do

  • No host permissions. Tallshot can only touch a page after you explicitly ask it to, and only that page.
  • No network code. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no remote configuration, and no server to talk to.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Captures are processed on your device and saved where you choose.
  • Settings only. The only thing Tallshot stores is the preferences on this page.
